What we do

A comparison platform — nothing more, nothing less

We connect you with independent UK engine suppliers. The supplier you choose handles the commercial relationship directly.

  • Help you compare quotes from vetted specialists
  • Provide transparent pricing guidance
  • Share marketplace data and useful context
  • We do not sell engines ourselves
  • We do not fit engines or provide warranties
  • We do not handle supplier payments
