Guidelines

Review guidelines

We publish all genuine reviews - positive or negative - as long as they:

  • Come from a verified customer who used our platform
  • Do not contain offensive language or personal attacks
  • Are relevant to the service we provide

We do not delete negative reviews.

We believe honest feedback - including criticism - makes our platform more trustworthy.

When a negative review appears, we:

  • Read it carefully
  • Investigate the issue (if the customer provides enough detail)
  • Reply professionally, acknowledging any legitimate concerns
Example response to a negative review:
“Thank you for your feedback. We are sorry to hear about your experience. A member of our team will be in touch to understand what went wrong and how we can improve.”
FAQ

Frequently asked questions about reviews

Are these reviews real and verified-
Yes. All customer reviews shown on this page come from real people who have used Engines Market. Supplier reviews come from businesses in our network. We do not invent or pay for fake reviews.
Why are there no negative reviews showing-
If you do not see negative reviews, it may be because the filter is set to “Highest rating” - try changing to “Lowest rating” or “Most recent”. We genuinely have far more positive reviews than negative ones (but we do receive occasional criticism, and we publish it).
Can I see reviews from a specific platform only-
Yes. Use the filter options above to show reviews from Trustpilot, Google, Facebook, or Reviews.io individually.
How do I leave a review if I am a supplier-
Please use the same public platforms (Google, Trustpilot, etc.) as customers. We monitor all of them. Alternatively, email your feedback to support@enginesmarket.co.uk and we may feature it (with your permission) in the supplier section.
