FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay for a quote-
No. The quote service is completely free for vehicle owners.
How long does it take to receive quotes-
Most users receive 3-5 quotes within a few hours. The average from enquiry to completed engine replacement is 5.2 days (based on our 2025 data).
What information do I need-
Your vehicle registration number (the API will pull make, model, and engine details) plus a brief description of the problem - e.g., “rattling noise on cold start”, “blue smoke”, “engine won’t start”.
Will you share my personal details-
Only with relevant suppliers - and only to enable them to quote. We do not sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
What if my car is very old or rare-
Our network covers a wide range of vehicles. Even for older or less common models, we will share your enquiry with specialists who are most likely to help.
