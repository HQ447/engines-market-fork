Before you accept any Quote or enter into any contract with a Supplier, you must take the following steps - and you do so at your own risk:

Step What to do
Verify the Supplier Check their company registration (Companies House), read their online reviews (Google, Trustpilot, Facebook), and search for any unresolved complaints.
Read the Supplier's terms Obtain and read the Supplier's own terms of sale, warranty policy, and any fitting/service terms before you agree to anything.
