Section 4

How we use your personal data (lawful bases)

Under UK GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for each processing activity. We rely on four lawful bases:

  • Contract — processing necessary to provide the service you requested.
  • Legitimate interests — processing that is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided your rights do not override those interests.
  • Legal obligation — processing required to comply with the law (e.g., tax, fraud prevention).
  • Consent — where you have given explicit permission (e.g., for non-essential cookies).
Purpose Lawful basis Explanation
To provide you with engine replacement quotes Contract Processing is necessary to perform our contract with you — we cannot provide quotes without sharing your data with suppliers.
To match your enquiry with relevant suppliers Contract Essential part of the service.
To manage our supplier network (onboarding, monitoring) Legitimate interests We have a legitimate interest in maintaining a high-quality supplier network. This does not override your rights because we use aggregated or anonymised data where possible.
To improve our Platform (analytics, A/B testing) Legitimate interests We have a legitimate interest in understanding how users interact with our Platform to improve performance. We use pseudonymised data where possible.
To secure our Platform (fraud prevention) Legitimate interests Protecting our business and users from security threats is a legitimate interest.
To comply with legal obligations Legal obligation We may be required to retain or disclose data by law.
To send service-related communications Contract These are part of the service you requested. You cannot opt out of service emails because they are necessary to deliver the service.

Important note on Legitimate Interests: We have conducted a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) for each purpose listed above. Our interests do not override your rights because we limit data sharing to what is necessary, we retain data only as long as required, and you can object at any time (see Section 6 — Your rights).

Section 5

How we share your personal data

5.1 Sharing with engine suppliers (core service)

When you submit a quote request, we share your personal data with our network of vetted UK engine suppliers. This is the fundamental purpose of our service.

What we share: Your name, email address, phone number, postcode, vehicle registration, make, model, engine code, mileage, and your description of the engine problem.

How the sharing works (safeguards):

Safeguard Description
Supplier cap A maximum of 10 suppliers can access your lead. Once 10 suppliers have viewed or quoted on your request, the lead is automatically closed and no further suppliers can access your data.
Data Sharing Agreement (DSA) Every supplier signs a binding DSA before joining our network. The DSA requires suppliers to: (i) act as an independent data controller; (ii) use your data only to provide a quote for the specific engine you requested; (iii) not add your data to any marketing database; (iv) not share your data with any third party; (v) indemnify us against any fines or claims resulting from their misuse of your data.
Supplier vetting Suppliers must be UK registered, have a physical business address, at least 12 months trading history, and a sustained average public review score of 4.0 stars or higher.
Monitoring and removal We monitor complaints and public reviews. If a supplier repeatedly breaches our terms or their rating falls below 3.5 stars, we remove them from our network.

Important: Once a supplier receives your data, they become an independent data controller for that data. Their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policy. We do not control how they use your data after that point, but we require them contractually to comply with UK data protection law. We strongly recommend you read the supplier's privacy policy before engaging their services.

5.2 Sharing with service providers (processors)

Service provider Purpose Data shared
Website hosting provider To store and serve our website Technical data (IP addresses, server logs)
CRM platform To manage leads and supplier interactions Name, email, phone, vehicle details, enquiry notes
Email delivery service To send you quote confirmations and follow-up emails Email address, name
Analytics provider (e.g., Google Analytics) To analyse website usage (pseudonymised) Pseudonymised IP address, browser data, page views
DVLA vehicle lookup API To retrieve vehicle details from your registration Vehicle registration number

5.3 Sharing for legal or regulatory reasons

We may disclose your personal data if required by law, court order, or a government authority (e.g., HMRC, police, ICO). We may also disclose data to enforce our terms and conditions, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our business, our users, or others.

5.4 Sharing with your consent

In any other situation, we will ask for your explicit consent before sharing your data.

Section 6

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data. They are free to exercise (except in limited circumstances).

Right Description How to exercise
Right of access You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. Email dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk with “SAR” in the subject line. We will respond within one month.
Right to rectification If your data is inaccurate or incomplete, you can ask us to correct it. Email dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk with the correct information.
Right to erasure You can ask us to delete your personal data. This is not absolute (e.g., we may need to retain data for legal reasons). Email dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk. We will delete your data within one month, unless a legal exception applies. There is no fee for erasure requests.
Right to restrict processing You can ask us to temporarily stop processing your data (e.g., while a dispute is resolved). Email dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk explaining why you want restriction.
Right to data portability You can request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g., CSV) to transfer to another service. Email dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk. We will provide your data within one month.
Right to object You can object to processing based on legitimate interests (e.g., analytics, service improvement). Email dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk. We will stop processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds to continue.
Right to withdraw consent If we ever rely on consent (e.g., for marketing or non-essential cookies), you can withdraw it at any time. Adjust your cookie preferences via our banner or email dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk.

To exercise any right, please contact: dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk

We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. We will respond within one calendar month (30 days). If your request is complex or you have made multiple requests, we may extend the response time by a further two months — we will inform you within the first month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you are unhappy with how we have handled your data. See Section 14 for contact details.

Section 8

International data transfers

Your personal data is primarily stored and processed within the United Kingdom.

Some of our service providers (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics) may operate servers outside the UK — typically in the European Economic Area (EEA) or other countries with adequate data protection laws.

When we transfer your data outside the UK, we ensure an equivalent level of protection by using:

  • The UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA) — the UK's replacement for the EU SCCs
  • Transfers to countries with UK adequacy decisions (e.g., the EEA, Japan, South Korea)

We do not transfer your data to countries without adequate protections unless we have your explicit consent or it is necessary for the performance of a contract.

If you want more information about our transfer mechanisms, please contact dpo@enginesmarket.co.uk.

Section 11

Image copyright — our commitment to intellectual property rights

Our Platform displays thousands of images of engines, vehicles, components, and related automotive content. We are committed to respecting intellectual property rights and complying with UK copyright law (Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988).

11.1 Sources of our images

Source Description Copyright status
Original photography Images we have taken ourselves (e.g., of reconditioning processes, workshops). © Engines Market / PRESTIGE AUTO ENGINEERING LTD. All rights reserved.
Supplier-provided images Images supplied to us by our network of engine specialists. We hold a perpetual, royalty-free licence from each supplier to use these images on our Platform. The supplier warrants that they have the right to provide the image.
AI-generated images Images created using artificial intelligence tools (e.g., DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion). These images are original works generated by AI at our direction. No human creator was directly involved. However, we acknowledge that AI-generated images may unintentionally resemble existing copyrighted works due to the nature of AI training data.

11.2 AI-generated images — disclaimer

We use AI-generated images for purposes such as illustrating engine types, vehicle models, failure examples, and generic workshop scenes. These images are not direct copies of any third-party photograph or illustration. Nevertheless, because AI models are trained on vast datasets, there is a small possibility that an AI-generated image may bear a resemblance to an existing copyrighted work.

If you believe an AI-generated image on our Platform infringes your copyright, please contact us using the process below. We take such claims seriously and will investigate promptly.

11.3 Copyright infringement reporting (takedown process)

If you believe that any image on our Platform infringes your copyright (whether the image was AI-generated, provided by a supplier, or otherwise), please send a copyright infringement notice to:

copyrights@enginesmarket.co.uk

Your notice must include:

  • Your full name and contact information (email address and postal address).
  • A clear identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed (e.g., a description and, if possible, a URL or copy of the original work).
  • The specific URL(s) on our Platform where the allegedly infringing image appears.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the image is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on their behalf.
  • Your electronic or physical signature.

We will acknowledge receipt of your notice within 5 working days and will investigate within 14 days. If we determine that the image infringes your copyright, we will:

  • Immediately remove or disable access to the image.
  • Notify the supplier (if the image was sourced from a supplier) or, for AI-generated images, adjust our generation prompts to avoid similar outputs in the future.

We will also keep a record of all copyright complaints for compliance and auditing purposes.

11.4 Repeat infringers

Engines Market has a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of suppliers or users who are repeat infringers of intellectual property rights.
