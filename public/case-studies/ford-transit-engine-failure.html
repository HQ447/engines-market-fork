Failure timeline

£1,200 in wasted repairs - and the same problems came back within 3 months.

The worn injectors were over-fuelling the engine. Unburnt diesel was washing the oil from the cylinder bores - a condition known as "bore wash." This wore the piston rings, causing excessive oil consumption. The excess fuel was also rapidly clogging the new DPF.

What the manager thought

"Replace the DPF and EGR. Standard high-mileage Transit issues."

What it actually was

Failed injectors over-fuelling for months. Killed the DPF and EGR. Wore the rings and bores. £1,200 wasted.

The uncertainty play

Bore wash creates risk beyond the visible cylinder scoring.

When the specialist removed the injectors and inspected the bores, they found scoring on cylinder 2 that could be felt with a fingernail. The excessive oil consumption from the worn rings meant the turbo had been running with reduced oil quality.

Lessons learned

If your 2.2 Duratorq Transit has black smoke - test the injectors first.

A £500 injector service could save a £3,800 engine. Treating symptoms with DPF and EGR replacement wastes money and time. For fleet operators, downtime is the real cost - an exchange reconditioned engine with a 2-day turnaround is almost always better value than a 10-day rebuild.
