The uncertainty play

The rebuild was not the only question. The remaining injectors were a ticking time bomb.

With one injector tip failed, the other three were of the same age and batch. If another tip detached, the engine would need another rebuild. The specialist recommended replacing all four.

Path A

Rebuild only the damaged cylinder — re-bore cylinder three, one oversize piston, leave other three cylinders and existing injectors.

£2,500

If another injector fails later: add £1,500–£2,500 for another rebuild.

Path B

Full rebuild — all four cylinders re-bored, four oversize pistons, new rings, bearings, full set of new injectors.

£4,000

Eliminates risk of future injector failure. Expected life: 200,000+ miles.

The owner chose Path B. For a Hilux intended to last another 200,000 miles, doing the job properly once made more sense than cutting corners. Key takeaway: when one injector fails catastrophically, replace all four.

Lessons learned

If your 1KD-FTV Hilux suffers an injector failure, replace all four injectors.

The commercial lesson is simple: injector failures are cheaper when caught early. Replacing all four injectors at the same time eliminates the risk of a second failure and another rebuild.
