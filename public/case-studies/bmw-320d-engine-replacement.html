Early warning

The whole disaster started with a cold-start rattle.

The rattle lasted only 1-2 seconds at first, then quietened. The owner assumed it was normal for a diesel. By 90,000 miles, the chain had snapped.

What owner thought“Probably fine for a diesel”

The car still drove normally, so the symptom did not feel urgent.

What it actually wasTensioner failing

The N47 tensioner was losing pressure, allowing the chain to stretch and slap.

Why it mattersSnapped chain = bent valves

Once the chain snaps, valves bend, rockers break, and the top end is destroyed.

If your N47 rattles on cold start, book a timing chain inspection within 7 days. Do not wait for the chain to snap.

The uncertainty play

The top-end rebuild was not the only question. Debris had circulated through the oil system.

With shattered guide pieces and metal particles throughout the oil system, the specialist could not fully know whether the turbocharger or bottom end had already been affected without further disassembly.

Path A

Top-end rebuild only — new valves, rockers, camshaft (if needed), timing chain kit. Bottom end remains untouched.

£2,200–£3,500

If debris caused unseen bottom-end damage, future failure is possible.

Path B

Full reconditioned engine.

£3,500–£5,500

Eliminates all risk of hidden bottom-end damage. Complete warranty coverage.

The owner chose Path A - a top-end rebuild for £2,800. The specialist confirmed the bottom end appeared undamaged. The owner accepted the small risk of future issues in exchange for saving £2,000-£3,000.
