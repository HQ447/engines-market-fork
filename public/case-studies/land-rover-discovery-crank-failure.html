Lessons learned

If your Discovery 3 TDV6 develops a rumble under load, do not ignore it.

The TDV6 is known for crankshaft bearing failure and snapped crankshafts. A rumble under load is often an early warning of bottom-end bearing wear. Ignoring it can lead to catastrophic crankshaft failure.
