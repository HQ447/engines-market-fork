The uncertainty play

Overheating creates risk beyond the visible head crack.

When the specialist removed the cylinder head, they found visible cracking between the exhaust ports on cylinders 2 and 3. The engine had overheated before the owner stopped driving - overheating can cause cylinder bores to distort or piston rings to lose tension.

Path A

Replace only the cylinder head and head gasket - keep existing bottom end and turbos.

£2,800

If bottom end fails later: add £2,000-£3,500. If turbos fail later: add £1,500-£2,500.

Path B

Full engine rebuild - new head, timing set, oil pump, inspect bottom end.

£4,500

Eliminates most post-repair risks. Full peace of mind.

The owner chose Path B - prioritising reliability for continued towing work. The CFCA has two turbochargers - both rely on clean oil. Overheating can cause oil to coke in the turbo oil feed lines.

Lessons learned

If your CFCA Transporter is used for towing, monitor coolant levels weekly.

White smoke on cold start or frequent coolant loss requires immediate investigation. The owner in this scenario waited 5,000 miles - the repair cost doubled as a result.

Coolant pressure test at first white smoke£50-£100

Early detection of head gasket or head failure before catastrophic damage.
