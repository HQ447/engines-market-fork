⚠️ Engines to approach with caution

Known high-risk diesel engines

These engines have well-documented failure modes that can lead to catastrophic engine failure. Proceed with extreme caution, especially when buying used.

BMW N47 (2007-2014) ⭐⭐
Timing chain stretch and tensioner failure (rear-mounted chain design) - catastrophic if ignored. Early warning: cold-start rattle. Pre-2014 N47 engines are the highest-risk category. [EM-OBSERVED]
EM requests (2025): 1,450 [EM-VERIFIED] · Applications: BMW 1/3/5 Series, X1, X3 · ULEZ: Euro 5 - check
Land Rover 306DT SDV6 ⭐⭐
Crankshaft bearing failure (tin-aluminium coating fatigue) - catastrophic. Cold-start knock is the early warning. Repairs are extremely expensive (£5,500-£9,500). All model years are at risk. [EM-OBSERVED]
EM requests (2025): 980 [EM-VERIFIED] · Applications: Range Rover Sport, Discovery 5, L405 · ULEZ: Euro 5/6 - varies
Land Rover 204DTD Ingenium Diesel (2015-2019) ⭐⭐
Timing chain tensioner failure (early models) · Oil dilution from DPF regeneration · EGR cooler leaks. Improved in 2019+ models. If buying a used Ingenium diesel, prioritise 2019+ models. [EM-OBSERVED]
EM requests (2025): 1,150 [EM-VERIFIED] · Applications: Discovery Sport, Evoque, Freelander 2 · ULEZ: Euro 6 - compliant

ULEZ compliance reference

Which engines are ULEZ compliant-

If you drive in London or other UK clean air zones, emission standard is critical. Euro 6 is the minimum for ULEZ compliance.

Emission Standard ULEZ Compliant- Typical Vehicles
Euro 6 (2015+)✅ YesBMW B47, VW EA288, Mercedes OM654, Land Rover Ingenium (post-2019)
Euro 5 (2009-2015)❌ No (charge applies)BMW N47, VW EA189, Mercedes OM651, Ford Duratorq
Euro 4 (2005-2009)❌ No (charge applies)BMW M57 (pre-2009), VW 1.9 TDI (pre-2009)

Check your specific vehicle's Euro standard before purchasing. Some Euro 6 diesels also require AdBlue for compliance.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about diesel engine reliability

What is the most reliable diesel engine in the UK- +
The BMW M57 (1998-2013) and VW 1.9 TDI (PD) (1996-2010) are widely considered the most reliable diesel engines in the UK. Both regularly exceed 250,000 miles with proper maintenance. For a modern ULEZ-compliant option, the BMW B47 (2014+) is the top choice.
Which diesel engine lasts the longest- +
The BMW M57 and VW 1.9 TDI are documented to exceed 250,000 miles regularly. The Toyota 1KD-FTV also achieves 250,000+ miles in commercial applications such as Hilux and Land Cruiser.
Are BMW diesel engines reliable- +
It depends on the engine. The BMW M57 and B47 are highly reliable. The N47 (2007-2014) has known timing chain issues - pre-2014 examples are the highest-risk category. The B47 addresses these issues and is ULEZ-compliant.
What is the most reliable VW diesel engine- +
The 1.9 TDI (PD) is the most reliable VW diesel ever made. For modern VAG vehicles, the 2.0 TDI EA288 (2015+) is more reliable than the EA189 it replaced and is Euro 6 compliant.
Are Toyota diesel engines reliable- +
Yes. Toyota claim rates are 8.78% (Warranty Group, 2025). The 1KD-FTV (3.0 D4D) is extremely robust, and the AD series (2.2 D-CAT) is generally reliable when maintained - though it has a known head gasket weakness.
What is the most reliable Mercedes diesel engine- +
The OM654 (2015+) is the most reliable modern Mercedes diesel. The OM651 (2008-2015) is also reliable but has known injector seal issues (Black Death) and timing chain wear.
Which diesel engine should I avoid- +
The Land Rover 306DT SDV6 has catastrophic crankshaft bearing failure - all model years are at risk. The BMW N47 (pre-2014) and Land Rover 204DTD Ingenium (pre-2019) also have significant timing chain risks.
What is the best ULEZ-compliant diesel engine- +
The BMW B47 (2014+) is the top choice for ULEZ compliance. Other strong options include the VW EA288 (2015+) and Mercedes OM654 (2015+). All are Euro 6 compliant.
How many miles can a diesel engine last- +
With proper maintenance, the most reliable diesel engines (BMW M57, VW 1.9 TDI, Toyota 1KD-FTV) can last 250,000+ miles. Modern engines like the BMW B47 and VW EA288 are capable of 150,000+ miles with good maintenance.
How is engine reliability measured- +
Our index combines replacement frequency (lower is better), known failure patterns (from our failure database), documented mileage achievements, and external warranty data from major providers like Warranty Direct and MotorEasy.
Is the Land Rover Ingenium diesel reliable- +
Pre-2019 Land Rover Ingenium diesel units have significant timing chain risk. The engine was improved in 2019+ models. If buying a used Ingenium diesel, prioritise 2019+ models or budget for chain replacement.
What is the best engine for high mileage- +
The BMW M57 and VW 1.9 TDI are the gold standard for high-mileage diesel. Both regularly exceed 250,000 miles with proper maintenance. The Toyota 1KD-FTV is also excellent for commercial high-mileage use.
