Frequently asked questions

Common questions about engine failures

What is the most common engine failure in the UK- +
Timing chain failure is the most common, accounting for 18% of failure-related quote requests [EM-VERIFIED]. The BMW N47 is the most frequently cited engine, followed by Land Rover Ingenium and Mercedes OM651.
Which engines have the most timing chain problems- +
The BMW N47 (1,450 requests) is the most requested engine for timing chain issues. Other common ones: Mercedes OM651, Land Rover Ingenium (204DTD), and BMW N57. Cold-start rattle is the key early warning.
What causes Land Rover SDV6 crankshaft failure- +
The Land Rover 306DT SDV6 suffers from crankshaft bearing fatigue, often due to oil starvation or bearing wear. Failure is catastrophic - cold-start knock is the early warning. Repair costs average £5,500-£9,500 [EM-QUOTE].
How can I prevent DPF clogging- +
85% of DPF failures could be prevented [EM-OBSERVED]. Prevention actions: regular motorway driving (to allow regeneration), avoid short urban trips, use proper fuel, and have the DPF serviced at 80k miles.
What is the most expensive engine failure to repair- +
Crankshaft/bearing failure (especially Land Rover SDV6) is the most costly, with bottom-end rebuilds averaging £5,500-£7,000 and full replacement reaching £6,500-£9,500 [EM-QUOTE]. Timing chain failures can also be expensive if they cause full engine damage.
What are the early warning signs of timing chain failure- +
The most important early warning is cold-start rattle - a rattling sound on startup that lasts a few seconds. Other signs: engine management light, poor performance, and metallic noises from the engine. Don't ignore these - early intervention can prevent catastrophic damage.
How reliable is the data in this report- +
The data is derived from 24,650 real quote requests processed through Engines Market in 2025, triangulated with customer descriptions, supplier notes, post-sale surveys, and validated against manufacturer TSBs and public forums (Honest John, Parkers). All figures are [EM-VERIFIED] unless otherwise noted.
What is the best way to avoid engine failure- +
Regular maintenance is the single biggest risk reducer. Follow manufacturer service intervals, use correct oil and fuel, pay attention to early warning signs (rattle, warning lights), and consider proactive inspections at key mileages (60k, 80k, 100k).
Which brands have the most engine failures- +
Based on request volumes, BMW (N47, B47, N57), Land Rover (SDV6, TDV6, Ingenium), and Mercedes (OM651, OM642) dominate the top failure lists. This reflects both engineering issues and large vehicle parc sizes. Premium brands often have higher repair costs.
What should I do if I hear a rattle on startup- +
If you hear a cold-start rattle (rattling sound lasting 1-3 seconds), have your timing chain inspected immediately. This is a key early warning for timing chain stretch or tensioner failure. Ignoring it can lead to catastrophic engine failure and costly replacement.
