Frequently asked questions

Common questions about the UK engine market

What is the size of the UK engine replacement market- +
The UK automotive aftermarket is valued at approximately £34 billion [THIRD-PARTY: IBISWorld, 2025]. The automotive remanufacturing sector contributes around £500 million to the UK economy [THIRD-PARTY: SMMT, 2025]. Based on 24,650 quote requests in 2025, the engine replacement segment is a significant and growing part of this market.
What percentage of engine replacements are reconditioned- +
70.45% of quote requests in 2025 were for reconditioned engines [EM-VERIFIED]. This represents 17,365 of 24,650 total requests. The reconditioned share has grown from 58% in 2020 to 70.45% in 2025 - a 12.45 percentage point increase over six years.
Which engine code is most requested- +
The BMW N47D20C is the most requested engine code with 1,450 requests in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED]. This represents 5.9% of all quote requests. The N47 is known for timing chain issues, particularly in pre-2014 models.
How much have repair costs increased- +
Average repair costs increased 8% year-on-year in 2025 [THIRD-PARTY]. Labour rates rose from £65.63 to £68.51 per hour (+4.4%) [THIRD-PARTY]. Routine repair bills have increased by up to 23% year-on-year [THIRD-PARTY], significantly outpacing general inflation.
What is the most common engine failure type- +
Timing chain failure is the most common failure type at 18% of all failure-related quote requests [EM-VERIFIED]. This is followed by DPF clogging (15%) and turbocharger failure (14%). BMW N47, Mercedes OM651, and Land Rover Ingenium are the most affected engines.
How much more does engine replacement cost in London- +
The same engine can cost £1,000+ more in London than in the North of England [EM-OBSERVED]. Labour rates in London & South East range from £110-£160/hr, compared to £65-£95/hr in the North. Many owners travel to Midlands or Northern specialists for significant savings.
How fast is the hybrid claim rate growing- +
Hybrid claims increased from 1.5% to 3.1% of all claims in one year - a 107% increase [THIRD-PARTY]. EV claims grew from 0.7% to 1.4% (+100%). This reflects the growing electric vehicle parc and the unique complexity of hybrid powertrains.
What is the largest engine warranty claim on record- +
The largest recorded engine warranty claim in the UK was £26,233 for a 2016 BMW [THIRD-PARTY: RAC Warranty, 2024]. Other high-value claims include a Jaguar F-Pace SVR at £23,000 (2025) and an Audi (2020) at £22,954 (2025).
What is the customer satisfaction rate for reconditioned engines- +
Post-sale surveys show a 92% satisfaction rate for reconditioned engine installations [EM-SURVEY]. Surveys were conducted 3-6 months after installation with a sample of 100+ customers, supplemented by 1,200+ public reviews.
