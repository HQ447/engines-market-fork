About the Most Replaced Engines UK 2026

This document presents the definitive ranking of which engines actually get replaced in the UK - based on 24,650 real engine replacement quote requests processed through EnginesMarket.co.uk between January and December 2025 [EM-VERIFIED].

This is not a reliability ranking. High replacement frequency may reflect large vehicle parc, age profile, or audience bias - not engineering quality. However, for used car buyers, this data shows exactly which engines are most frequently requiring replacement and what to budget for.

Dataset Limitations

Understanding the data

  • Replacement frequency ≠ unreliability: High volume may reflect large vehicle parc, not engineering quality.
  • Self-reporting bias: Requests come from platform users, not a random sample.
  • Age skew: Older vehicles (8+ years) are overrepresented.
  • Commercial vehicles: Light commercial vehicles have different failure patterns and are not fully captured.
Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the most replaced engines

What is the most replaced engine in the UK- +
The BMW N47D20C is the most replaced engine in the UK, with 1,450 quote requests in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED]. This 2.0-litre diesel engine is found in BMW 3 Series, 1 Series, 5 Series, X1, and X3 models. The primary failure is timing chain stretch, particularly on the rear-mounted chain.
Is the most replaced engine the most unreliable- +
No. High replacement frequency may reflect large vehicle parc (many on the road), age profile, or audience bias - not engineering quality. For example, the VW EA189 2.0 TDI is #4 with 1,050 requests, partly because millions were sold in the UK.
How much does it cost to replace a BMW N47 engine- +
A reconditioned BMW N47D20C costs £3,200-£5,500 supply-only, or £3,850-£6,410 fitted [EM-QUOTE]. Used options start from £1,800-£3,200 supply-only. Fitting costs depend on your region and garage.
Why are Land Rover engines so prominent in the top 15- +
Land Rover engines occupy 4 of the top 10 spots (204DTD, 306DT SDV6, 276DT, 368DT). This is due to a combination of known engineering issues (timing chain tensioners, crankshaft bearings, oil pump housings), high vehicle values that justify replacement, and a large premium SUV parc in the UK.
Is it worth replacing a Ford DV6 1.6 TDCi engine- +
The Ford DV6 1.6 TDCi is flagged as a 🔴 Scrap Threshold engine. With fitted costs of £1,590-£2,720 and typical vehicle values of £1,200-£5,000, replacement often exceeds the car's value. Consider scrapping unless the car is in excellent condition.
Why does reconditioned cost more than used- +
Reconditioned engines are disassembled, cleaned, and have worn parts replaced (pistons, rings, bearings, seals). They are tested to standard and come with a 6-12 month warranty. Used engines are direct from donor vehicles with unknown history.
What should I consider when deciding repair vs. scrap- +
If the fitted replacement cost exceeds approximately 60% of the vehicle's current market value, consider scrapping. However, rare, collectable, or sentimental vehicles may justify spending above this threshold. Check our engine replacement cost guide for more guidance.
