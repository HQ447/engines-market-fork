Frequently asked questions

Common questions about UK engine replacement statistics

How many engine replacements were requested in the UK in 2025- +
24,650 real engine replacement quote requests were processed through Engines Market between January and December 2025 [EM-VERIFIED]. This represents a significant and growing market segment within the UK automotive aftermarket.
What percentage of engine replacements are reconditioned vs. used- +
70.45% reconditioned · 29.55% used [EM-VERIFIED]. Reconditioned engines have grown from 58% in 2020 to 70.45% in 2025, reflecting increased vehicle complexity, higher quality standards, and warranty preference.
What is the most commonly replaced engine in the UK- +
The BMW N47D20C is the most requested engine code with 1,450 quote requests in 2025, representing 5.9% of all requests. It's commonly found in BMW 3 Series, 1 Series, 5 Series, X1, and X3 models. [EM-VERIFIED]
How long does an engine replacement take on average- +
The average timeline from quote request to completion is 5.2 days [EM-VERIFIED]. Fitting labour typically takes 1-3 days depending on engine complexity, with 2-5 days for engine sourcing and delivery.
What is the customer satisfaction rate for reconditioned engines- +
92% customer satisfaction based on post-installation surveys conducted 3-6 months after replacement [EM-SURVEY]. Surveys cover performance, reliability, noise/vibration, and overall satisfaction. Sample size: 100+ surveys, supplemented by 1,200+ public reviews.
Which vehicle age group requests the most engine replacements- +
Vehicles aged 10-15 years and 15+ years each account for 35-40% of quote requests [EM-OBSERVED]. Vehicles under 5 years account for less than 5% of requests, as they are typically still under manufacturer warranty.
How much does the average engine repair cost- +
The average engine-related repair bill has increased to £679, up 8% year-on-year [THIRD-PARTY: Industry Workshop Surveys, 2025]. Labour rates have risen 4.4% to £68.51/hr, and routine repairs are up 23% year-on-year.
How are EV and hybrid engine claims changing- +
EV and hybrid claims more than doubled in 2025, growing 107% year-on-year [THIRD-PARTY: Warranty Group Industry Report, 2025]. While still a small segment (3.1% of all claims), this represents significant growth potential for the automotive aftermarket.
What is the source of this data- +
This dataset is derived from 24,650 real engine replacement quote requests processed through Engines Market between January and December 2025. All figures are anonymised and aggregated. Sources are marked [EM-VERIFIED], [EM-QUOTE], [EM-SURVEY], [EM-OBSERVED], [THIRD-PARTY], or [INDUSTRY ESTIMATE].
What are the limitations of this dataset- +
Limitations include: sample bias (platform users may not represent the broader population), regional weighting (London/SE overrepresented vs. rural areas), vehicle age skew (older vehicles overrepresented, consistent with engine failure patterns), and preventative replacements (estimated <5% of requests).
