What's included in labour vs extras

Understanding what you're paying for

✅ Typically included

  • ✓ Labour time
  • ✓ Removal of old component
  • ✓ Fitting of new component
  • ✓ Basic testing
  • ✓ Disposal of old parts (usually)
  • ✓ Basic clean-up

❌ Typically excluded

  • ✗ Consumables (oil, coolant, filters) - these are parts
  • ✗ Diagnostics - time spent diagnosing (£50-£150/hour)
  • ✗ Hire car - courtesy vehicle or hire car
  • ✗ Disposal - some garages charge for disposal
  • ✗ Gaskets and seals - often extra (£20-£100)
💡 Always ask:

"What's included in the labour price-" Some garages quote labour-only, others quote labour + consumables. This can make a significant difference to the total cost.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about garage labour rates

What's the average garage labour rate in the UK- +
The average garage labour rate in the UK is £68.51 per hour [THIRD-PARTY]. However, rates vary significantly by region and garage type: independent garages £55-£120/hour, main dealers £90-£220/hour, specialists £80-£160/hour.
Why do labour rates vary so much across the UK- +
Labour rates vary by up to 200% across regions due to: cost of living (higher in London/SE), property costs (higher rents), staff costs (higher wages), competition, and demand. Scotland and the North are significantly cheaper than London and the South East.
What's the difference between independent, main dealer, and specialist rates- +
Independent garages: £55-£120/hour (best value). Main dealers: £90-£220/hour (most expensive). Specialists: £80-£160/hour (mid-range, specific make knowledge). Independents are typically 40-60% cheaper than main dealers.
What's included in the labour price- +
Labour typically includes: removal of old component, fitting of new component, basic testing, and clean-up. It typically excludes: consumables (oil, coolant, filters), diagnostics, hire car, and sometimes disposal of old parts.
How many hours does an engine replacement take- +
Engine replacement takes 8-24 hours depending on vehicle type. FWD 4-cylinder: 8-12 hours. RWD 4-cylinder: 10-15 hours. 4WD SUV: 12-20 hours. V6/V8 4WD: 16-24 hours.
How can I save on labour costs- +
Compare independent quotes (40-60% saving), travel outside London/SE (£400-£1,000 saving), bundle jobs (e.g., clutch with gearbox), use supply and fit package (5-15% saving), and avoid main dealers unless necessary.
Is it worth travelling outside London for cheaper labour- +
Yes - choosing a specialist outside London or the South East can save £400-£1,000 on labour for a typical engine replacement. Many owners travel to Midlands or Northern specialists for significant savings without compromising quality.
What's the labour time for a timing chain replacement- +
Timing chain replacement takes 8-18 hours depending on chain location. Front chain: 8-12 hours. Rear chain (BMW N47/N57): 12-18 hours (engine or gearbox removal required).
What's the labour time for a head gasket repair- +
Head gasket repair takes 8-22 hours depending on engine configuration. 4-cylinder: 8-15 hours. V6: 12-18 hours. V8: 15-22 hours.
What's the labour time for a turbo replacement- +
Turbo replacement takes 3-15 hours depending on single vs twin turbo. Single turbo: 3-8 hours. Twin turbo: 8-15 hours. Some turbos are at the front (easy), others at the back (difficult).
What's the labour time for a gearbox replacement- +
Gearbox replacement takes 5-15 hours depending on gearbox type. Manual: 5-8 hours. Automatic: 7-12 hours. DSG/DCT: 8-15 hours. 4WD adds complexity.
What's the labour time for a clutch replacement- +
Clutch replacement takes 4-8 hours. FWD: 4-6 hours. RWD: 5-7 hours. 4WD: 6-8 hours. Often done with gearbox replacement - labour is saved.
What's labour's share of total engine replacement cost- +
Labour accounts for 40-60% of total engine replacement cost. For a typical engine replacement (£2,900-£4,900), labour is £800-£1,400 (25-35%). For more complex jobs, labour's share is higher.
Should I use a mobile mechanic- +
Mobile mechanics are cheaper (£50-£90/hour) than garage-based mechanics. However, they're only suitable for smaller jobs (injectors, sensors, basic maintenance). For engine replacement or head gasket repair, a workshop is required.
