Injector replacement cost summary

What does injector replacement cost in the UK-

The short answer: Injector replacement costs range from £120 to £900+ per injector depending on fuel type. Petrol injectors are cheaper (£120-£500 per injector), while diesel injectors cost more (£300-£900+ per injector). For a full set of four diesel injectors, expect £800-£2,000+. Reconditioned diesel injectors cost £90-£190 each, while new replacements cost £250-£600+.

The longer answer: For most UK drivers, injector replacement is a common and often misunderstood expense. Here's what you can expect:

Petrol (single) £120-£500 fitted

Signs of injector failure

How to spot failing fuel injectors

🔄 Rough idleInjector not delivering fuel correctly at low RPM - have it checked
💥 MisfireInjector not firing or leaking - have it checked immediately
⛽ Poor fuel economyInjector leaking or over-fuelling - have it checked, costs you money
🖤 Black smokeOver-fuelling - too much fuel being injected - have it checked immediately
🔥 Fuel smellInjector leaking fuel externally - STOP DRIVING - fire risk
🚨 Engine management lightInjector fault codes - diagnostic check required
📉 Loss of powerInjector not delivering enough fuel - have it checked
🔑 Hard startingInjector leaking or not firing - have it checked

Critical warning: If you smell fuel, stop driving immediately. This indicates an external fuel leak, which is a fire risk. Have the vehicle recovered to a garage.

Cleaning vs repair vs replacement

Understanding your options

🧼 Injector Cleaning
£50-£150
Ultrasonic cleaning to remove deposits and restore spray pattern. 6-12 months warranty. Best for clogged injectors.
🔧 Injector Repair
£80-£200
Strip down, inspect, replace worn parts (seals, springs, nozzles). 6-12 months warranty. Best for worn seals, minor internal wear.
🔄 Reconditioned (Exchange)
£90-£200
Fully rebuilt, recalibrated, and tested to factory spec. 12-24 months warranty. Best for most owners - best value.
🆕 New Injector
£120-£900+
Brand new from OEM or aftermarket. 12-24 months warranty. Best for insurance claims, high-value vehicles.

Why diesel injectors cost more: Higher fuel pressure (1,500-2,500 bar), more complex internals, seizure risk in cylinder head, must be coded to ECU, and require calibration. Diesel injectors often seize - removal is difficult and time-consuming.

Diesel injector coding

Why diesel injectors need coding

When replacing a diesel injector, the new injector must be coded to the ECU. Each injector has a unique calibration code that tells the ECU how much fuel to deliver.

What is coding-
Entering the injector's calibration code into the ECU using specialist diagnostic equipment (e.g., VAG-COM, BMW ISTA, Mercedes STAR).
Why it's needed
Ensures correct fuel delivery - without coding, the engine may run poorly, fuel economy suffers, and the injector may fail prematurely. Usually included in labour or £50-£100 extra.
❌ What happens if coding isn't done
  • Engine runs poorly
  • Fuel economy suffers
  • Injector may fail prematurely
  • Engine management light may come on
  • Vehicle may fail emissions tests
✅ What happens when coding is done
  • ECU knows exactly how much fuel each injector delivers
  • Fuel is delivered precisely
  • Engine runs smoothly
  • Fuel economy is optimised
  • Emissions are minimised

Before you agree

10 questions to ask before replacing your injectors

1. "Is this cleaning, repair, or replacement-"

Understand what you're paying for. Cleaning is cheapest, repair is mid-range, replacement is most expensive. Don't pay for replacement if cleaning would suffice.

2. "What's included in the price-"

Get a full written breakdown. Ask about: injector, seals, labour, coding (diesel), and testing.

3. "Is this a new, reconditioned, or used injector-"

New is most expensive, reconditioned is best value, used is cheapest but riskiest. Most owners choose reconditioned.

4. "What warranty do you offer on the replacement-"

Typical warranty: reconditioned 12-24 months, new 12-24 months, used 1-3 months. Get it in writing.

5. "Will you code the injector to the ECU-" (diesel)

Diesel injectors must be coded to the ECU. Ask if this is included and what tool is used.

6. "What happens if the injector is seized-"

Diesel injectors often seize in the cylinder head. Ask about the removal process and potential additional costs.

7. "Is a fuel filter change included-"

Fuel filters are often changed when replacing injectors. Ask if this is included or an extra.

8. "What's the timeline from booking to completion-"

Single injector replacement typically takes 1-2 days. Full set takes 2-3 days.

9. "Do you have experience with my engine type-"

Some engines have known injector issues. Ask about their experience with your specific engine.

10. "Can you provide a written quote-"

Get everything in writing before agreeing. The scope of work, cost, timeline, and warranty terms should all be documented.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about injector replacement

How much does injector replacement cost in the UK- +
Injector replacement costs range from £120 to £900+ per injector depending on fuel type. Petrol injectors cost £120-£500 per injector, while diesel injectors cost £300-£900+ per injector. For a full set of four diesel injectors, expect £800-£2,000+. Reconditioned diesel injectors cost £90-£190 each, while new replacements cost £250-£600+.
What are the signs of a failing injector- +
Rough idle, misfire, poor fuel economy, black smoke, fuel smell, engine management light, loss of power, and hard starting. Rough idle and misfire are the most common early signs. Fuel smell means stop driving immediately (fire risk).
What's the difference between petrol and diesel injectors- +
Diesel injectors operate at much higher pressures (1,500-2,500 bar) and are more complex. They also require coding to the ECU. This makes diesel injectors more expensive to replace - £300-£900 vs £120-£500 for petrol.
Can I drive with a failing injector- +
If the injector is leaking externally (fuel smell) - stop driving immediately (fire risk). If it's just rough running or poor fuel economy, you may be able to drive to a garage, but don't delay. Continuing to drive can damage the catalytic converter (petrol), DPF (diesel), and engine.
Should I replace one injector or all of them- +
If one injector has failed and the others are in good condition, replacing just the failed one is acceptable. However, if the injectors are high-mileage, it may be worth replacing all of them to prevent future failures. A specialist will advise.
What's the difference between cleaning, repair, and replacement- +
Cleaning: Ultrasonic cleaning to remove deposits (£50-£150). Repair: Strip down and replace worn parts (£80-£200). Replacement: New or reconditioned injector (£120-£900+). Cleaning is cheapest but only suitable for clogged injectors. Replacement is most expensive but ensures reliability.
Do diesel injectors need coding- +
Yes - diesel injectors must be coded to the ECU. Each injector has a unique calibration code that tells the ECU how much fuel to deliver. Without coding, the engine runs poorly. Coding is usually included in labour or costs £50-£100 extra.
How long does injector replacement take- +
Typical replacement time is 1-3 days. Single petrol injector: 1-2 hours (1 day with parts). Single diesel injector: 2-4 hours (1-2 days if seized). Full set: 1-3 days.
Can I replace injectors myself- +
Injector replacement is not a DIY job for most owners. It requires specialist tools (injector pullers for diesel), diagnostic equipment (coding for diesel), and careful handling. Incorrect installation can cause the injector to fail or leak. Use a specialist.
What's the most common injector failure- +
The most common failure is clogged nozzles (petrol) - caused by poor fuel quality or infrequent fuel filter changes. For diesel, the most common failure is electrical failure (internal solenoid) or mechanical failure (worn nozzle). Diesel injectors also often seize in the cylinder head.
Should I replace the fuel filter when replacing injectors- +
Yes - strongly recommended. The fuel filter can be clogged with debris, which may damage the new injectors. Most specialists include a fuel filter change or recommend it.
What happens if I ignore a failing injector- +
Ignoring failing injectors leads to unburnt fuel washing cylinder walls, fuel diluting engine oil, catalytic converter/DPF damage, and eventually catastrophic engine failure. Repair costs escalate from £120-£900 to £4,000-£10,000 for engine replacement.
Can injectors be reconditioned- +
Yes - diesel injectors can be reconditioned (exchanged) for £90-£190 each. Petrol injectors can also be reconditioned but are often replaced due to lower replacement cost. Reconditioned injectors offer like-new performance at a fraction of the cost of new.
Does injector replacement affect insurance- +
Any major mechanical work, including injector replacement, should be declared to your insurer. The work may affect vehicle value and should be recorded.
