Signs of crankshaft failure

How to spot crankshaft problems

Crankshaft failure is a worst-case scenario. Here's what to look for - and what to do.

⚠️ The ticking clock:

A knocking crank is a ticking time bomb. Continuing to drive will: spin a bearing → weld the bearing to the crank → snap the connecting rod → window the block → destroy the engine. Early diagnosis costs £800-£1,500. Catastrophic failure costs £2,700-£10,000+.

Repair types - understanding your options

Your options explained

Bearing replacement
£800-£1,500
Engine removed, sump taken off, bearings replaced. Crank journals inspected but not machined. ✅ Viable - if caught early and crank journals are not damaged.
Crankshaft machining (regrind)
£1,200-£2,000
Crank removed and ground to a smaller size; oversize bearings fitted. ✅ Viable - if crank is not cracked or severely damaged.
Complete crankshaft replacement
£2,500-£5,000+
Old crank removed, replaced with new or reconditioned crank. ⚠️ Marginal - often more cost-effective to replace the entire engine.
Engine replacement
£2,700-£10,000+
Engine removed and replaced with used, reconditioned, or remanufactured engine. ✅ Usually the most cost-effective - if the crank is damaged.

Vehicle value rule

Repair vs replace vs scrap - the decision framework

If the cost of repair or replacement exceeds 50-60% of the vehicle's market value, consider scrapping. Rare, collectable, or sentimental vehicles may justify spending above this threshold.

Under £5,000🟢 Scrap or sell for parts - repair costs exceed vehicle value.
£5,000-£8,000🟡 Evaluate carefully - repair may be marginal; engine replacement often not cost-effective.
£8,000-£15,000🟠 Consider engine replacement - if vehicle is otherwise excellent.

Before you agree

10 questions to ask before crankshaft repair

1. Is the crank damaged or is it just the bearings-

If the crank is not damaged, bearing replacement is cheaper. If it's scored or cracked, the cost escalates significantly.

2. Can the crank be machined or does it need replacing-

If scored but not cracked, machining (regrind) is possible. If cracked or severely damaged, replacement is required.

3. What's the condition of the rest of the engine-

If high-mileage and the crank is damaged, engine replacement may be more cost-effective.

4. What's the total cost including all parts and labour-

Get a full written breakdown. Ask about potential additional costs.

5. What warranty do you offer on the repair-

Typical warranty is 12-24 months on parts and labour. Get it in writing.

6. What's the timeline from booking to completion-

Engine replacement or crank repair takes 1-3 weeks. Machining takes 2-4 weeks.

7. Is the vehicle worth repairing-

Ask the specialist's honest opinion. If under £5,000-£8,000, repair may not be economical.

8. What caused the failure-

Oil pump issue, bearing wear, or something else- If you don't address the root cause, the repair will fail again.

9. Do you have experience with my engine type-

Some engines (Land Rover TDV6, BMW N57) have known crank issues and require specialist knowledge.

10. Can you provide a written quote-

Get everything in writing before agreeing. Scope, cost, timeline, and warranty terms.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about crankshaft repair costs

How much does crankshaft repair cost in the UK- +
Crankshaft repair costs range from £800 to £5,000+ depending on the extent of damage. Bearing replacement costs £800-£1,500, crank machining costs £1,200-£2,000, and complete crank replacement costs £2,500-£5,000+. However, if the crank is damaged, engine replacement (£2,700-£10,000+) is usually more economical.
What are the signs of crankshaft failure- +
Deep knocking noise (rhythmic, increases with revs), low oil pressure warning, metal particles in oil, excessive engine vibration, engine stalls, and oil pressure light. Deep knocking is the most common sign - stop driving immediately.
Can I drive with a knocking crank- +
No - stop driving immediately. A knocking crank means the bearings are failing. Continuing to drive will damage the crank, snap the connecting rod, and destroy the engine. The repair cost will escalate from £800-£1,500 to £2,700-£10,000+.
What's the difference between bearing replacement and crank replacement- +
Bearing replacement (£800-£1,500) replaces only the bearings - the crank is not damaged. Crank replacement (£2,500-£5,000+) replaces the crankshaft itself - the crank is damaged. If the crank is damaged, engine replacement (£2,700-£10,000+) is often more cost-effective.
Which engines are most at risk of crankshaft failure- +
Land Rover 306DT 3.0 TDV6 (2005-2016) - VERY HIGH risk. BMW N57 3.0 diesel (2008-2016) - HIGH risk. Land Rover Ingenium 2.0 (2015-2020) - HIGH risk. Ford 2.0 EcoBlue (2016-2020) - HIGH risk. These engines have known crankshaft bearing issues.
Is crankshaft repair worth it on an older car- +
If the vehicle is worth under £5,000-£8,000, repair is rarely economical. If the vehicle is worth £8,000-£15,000, evaluate carefully. If the vehicle is worth £15,000+, repair or replacement is viable. The rule of thumb: if repair exceeds 50-60% of vehicle value, consider scrapping.
What happens if I ignore a knocking crank- +
The bearings will fail, the crank will be scored or cracked, the connecting rod will snap, and the rod will exit the block (windowed block). The engine will be destroyed - replacement required (£2,700-£10,000+). Early diagnosis (bearing replacement) costs £800-£1,500.
Can a crank be repaired or does it need replacing- +
If the crank is scored but not cracked, it can be machined (reground) for £1,200-£2,000. If the crank is cracked or severely damaged, it must be replaced (£2,500-£5,000+). If the crank is damaged, engine replacement (£2,700-£10,000+) is often more cost-effective.
How long does crankshaft repair take- +
Bearing replacement: 1-2 weeks. Crank machining: 2-4 weeks. Crank replacement: 2-4 weeks. Engine replacement: 1-2 weeks. Machining takes the longest due to sending the crank to a machine shop.
What's the most common cause of crankshaft failure- +
Oil pump failure (BMW N57, Land Rover TDV6), oil starvation (low oil level, infrequent changes), bearing wear (high mileage), and manufacturing defects (some engines). Regular oil changes and checking oil level prevent many failures.
Can I repair a crankshaft myself- +
No - crankshaft repair requires specialist knowledge, tools, and equipment. The engine must be removed, stripped, and reassembled correctly. Incorrect assembly will cause the repair to fail. Use a specialist.
Does crankshaft repair affect insurance- +
Any major mechanical work, including crankshaft repair or engine replacement, should be declared to your insurer. The work may affect vehicle value and should be recorded.
What's the difference between a spun bearing and a scored crank- +
A spun bearing is when the bearing seizes and rotates with the crank - this damages the crank journal. A scored crank is when the crank journal is scratched or grooved by debris or bearing material. Both require machining or replacement.
What's a "windowed block"- +
A windowed block is when the connecting rod punches a hole through the engine block. This is catastrophic failure - the engine is scrap and cannot be repaired. Replacement is required.
