Symptoms

Signs You Need Engine Diagnostics

If you notice any of these symptoms, a professional diagnostic can identify the root cause before you spend money on unnecessary repairs.

⚠️ Engine management light on
Sensor fault, emissions issue, internal problem
⚠️ Get checked
⚠️ Loss of power / limp mode
Turbo issue, injector failure, DPF blockage
⚠️ Get checked soon
⚠️ Rough idle / misfire
Ignition issue, injector failure, vacuum leak
⚠️ Get checked
🔴 Knocking or rattling noise
Timing chain, bearing, or bottom-end issue
🔴 Get checked immediately
⚠️ Excessive smoke (black/blue/white)
Injector issue, turbo failure, head gasket
⚠️ Get checked
🔴 Overheating
Thermostat, water pump, head gasket
🔴 Stop driving, get checked
🔴 Oil warning light
Low oil pressure, oil pump failure
🔴 Stop driving immediately
⚠️ Coolant warning light
Low coolant, leak, head gasket
⚠️ Get checked

When to Get Diagnostics Immediately

Knocking noise from engineCould be bearing failure - driving could destroy the engine.

Engine seizedComplete failure - diagnostics determine if repair is possible.
Major smoke / loss of powerCould be turbo or injector failure - driving could cause further damage.

After Diagnostics - Your Options

Once you have the diagnostic report, you'll know exactly what's wrong. Here's how to decide what to do next.

✅ Repairable Isolated failure, no bottom-end damage, no metal in oil, repair cost reasonable (under 50% of vehicle value).

Example: Single injector, sensor, or component failure.
Get repair quotes →
🔄 Replacement Needed Catastrophic failure, bottom-end damage, metal in oil, repair cost unreasonable (exceeds 50% of vehicle value).

Example: Snapped timing chain, seized engine, bearing failure.
Get replacement quotes →
❓ Unclear / Further Investigation Intermittent fault, multiple possible causes, complex system fault.

Example: Fault appears sometimes, ECU or wiring issue, several components could be causing the symptom.
Book advanced inspection →

Frequently asked questions

Engine Diagnostics FAQs

How much does engine diagnostics cost- +
Basic OBD2 scan £50-£100, full system diagnostic £100-£200, advanced inspection £200-£400, specialist investigation £250-£500. Prices vary by region and specialist.
What does engine diagnostics include- +
OBD2 trouble code retrieval, code interpretation, live data analysis, component testing, and a written report with findings, recommendations, and cost estimates.
Do I need diagnostics before repairing or replacing- +
Yes - diagnostics reveal what's actually wrong. Without diagnostics, you risk spending money on parts or repairs that don't fix the problem.
What happens after diagnostics- +
If the issue is repairable, get repair quotes. If replacement is needed, get replacement quotes. If unclear, book advanced investigation. Get repair quotes → Get replacement quotes →
Can I do diagnostics myself- +
You can buy an OBD2 reader and scan your own codes. However, interpreting the codes correctly requires expertise. A professional diagnostic includes live data, component testing, and a written report.
What if the diagnostic says my engine needs replacing- +
If the diagnostic confirms catastrophic failure, get replacement quotes - used, reconditioned, rebuilt. Replacement is usually the best option for snapped chains, bearing failures, or seized engines.
What if the diagnostic says my engine needs repair- +
If the diagnostic confirms an isolated failure, get repair quotes. Timing chain £650-£1,800, head gasket £800-£2,000, turbo £700-£2,500, injectors £400-£1,200.
Do I need to tell the DVLA and my insurer about diagnostics- +
No - diagnostics are testing only. If repairs or replacement are performed, you may need to notify the DVLA and your insurer.
