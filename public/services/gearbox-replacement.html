Symptoms

Signs Your Gearbox Needs Attention

If you notice any of these symptoms, get it checked early to avoid costly replacement.

Grinding into gear
Synchromesh wear, low fluid, clutch issue (manual)
Inspect, likely repair or replacement needed
Slipping out of gear
Worn synchromesh, damaged shift forks
Replacement likely
Delayed engagement
Low fluid, worn clutch packs (auto), solenoid issue
Check fluid level; may need repair or replacement
Harsh shifting
Valve body issue (auto), worn components
Inspect valve body; may need repair or replacement
Whining or humming noise
Worn bearings, low fluid
Inspect; repair or replacement likely
Transmission warning light
Internal fault detected - fluid temp, solenoid, sensor
Diagnostic scan; repair or replacement needed
Burning smell
Overheated fluid - clutch packs slipping (auto)
Stop driving; replacement likely
Leaking fluid
Seals worn, damaged casing
Inspect; repair or replacement depending on damage

When to Stop Driving Immediately

Transmission warning lightInternal fault - driving could cause catastrophic damage.
Burning smellFluid overheating - clutch packs are slipping.
Complete loss of driveMajor internal failure - driving is impossible.
Loud grinding / clunkingInternal damage - driving could destroy the gearbox.
"The most common mistake we see is customers ignoring early warning signs - a slight delay in engagement, a gentle whine. By the time they bring it in, the damage is extensive and replacement is the only option. If you notice anything unusual, get it checked early."

Decision framework

Repair vs Replacement Decision Framework

Use this framework to decide whether to repair or replace your gearbox.

✅ Slight delay in engagement, no noise, fluid level low
Repair - top up fluid, check for leaks
✅ Harsh shifting, no warning light, fluid dirty
Repair - fluid change, valve body service may fix
⚠️ Grinding into gear, popping out of gear
Replace - internal wear, synchromesh damage
❌ Complete loss of drive, loud noises, warning light on
Replace - catastrophic internal failure
❌ Vehicle value under £2,000, replacement cost over £1,500
Scrap or used - replacement is uneconomical
✅ Vehicle value over £5,000, replacement cost under £2,000
Repair or replace - cost is proportionate

The Threshold Rule

Under 30%✅ Repair - financially sound
30-50%⚠️ Evaluate carefully - consider vehicle value
Over 50%❌ Replace - repair is approaching replacement cost

Frequently asked questions

Gearbox Replacement FAQs

How much does gearbox replacement cost- +
Manual gearbox replacement £500-£3,000, automatic £1,000-£4,000, CVT £1,200-£5,000, DSG/DCT £1,500-£7,000. Prices vary by gearbox type, condition, and vehicle.
What's the difference between used, reconditioned, and new- +
Used - removed from donor vehicle, sold as-is. Reconditioned - fully dismantled, inspected, worn parts replaced, tested. New - brand new from manufacturer, most expensive.
Should I repair or replace my gearbox- +
If the issue is caught early - a delay in engagement, harsh shifting - repair may be possible. If you have grinding, popping out of gear, or complete loss of drive, replacement is likely needed.
What gearbox type do I have- +
If VW/Audi/Seat/Skoda - likely DSG. If Japanese (Honda/Toyota/Nissan) - likely CVT or automatic. If premium (BMW/Mercedes/Land Rover) - likely automatic (torque converter). If performance - likely DSG/DCT.
Should I replace the clutch at the same time- +
If your gearbox is being replaced - yes. The labour is already being done, so you'll save money by replacing the clutch now rather than later.
How long does gearbox replacement take- +
Manual: 4-8 hours (1 day). Automatic: 8-15 hours (1-2 days). DSG/DCT: 10-15 hours (2-3 days). Additional time for sourcing the gearbox.
What warranty comes with a replacement gearbox- +
Used: 1-6 months. Reconditioned: 3-12 months. New: 12-24 months. Always check the specific terms with your supplier.
Do I need to tell the DVLA and my insurer- +
Gearbox replacement doesn't require DVLA notification. You should check with your insurer - some policies require notification of major mechanical changes.
