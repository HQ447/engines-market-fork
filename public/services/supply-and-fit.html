Symptoms

Signs You Need a Complete Engine Replacement

Before you commit to a supply-and-fit engine replacement, it's worth understanding the signs that indicate your engine cannot be repaired and needs a complete replacement.

🔊
Deep knocking or rumbling from engine
Rod bearing or main bearing failure - bottom-end damage is usually terminal.
❌ Replace
🛑
Complete engine seizure
Catastrophic internal failure - no oil pressure, rod through block - engine is scrap.
❌ Replace
⛓️
Snapped timing belt/chain on interference engine
Valves have hit pistons - extensive damage. Repair often costs more than replacement.
❌ Replace
🔄
Crankshaft bearing failure
Spun bearing, damaged crankshaft, debris in oil system - bearing failure often destroys the block.
❌ Replace
🌡️
Persistent overheating with oil contamination
Head gasket failure, cracked block or cylinder head - may be repairable if caught early.
⚠️ Evaluate
⚙️
Metal shavings in oil
Internal wear - bearings, rings, cylinder bores - internal damage is widespread.
❌ Replace
"Most of the engines we replace are beyond economic repair. When you're looking at a snapped timing chain, a spun bearing, or a seized engine, the cost of repair often exceeds the cost of replacement - and you still don't know what else is damaged inside. Supply-and-fit gives you a clean slate."

What's Included in Supply and Fit

When you choose a supply-and-fit service, you're getting a complete turnkey solution - not just an engine.

Professional installation of the replacement unit.

Testing
Post-installation testing - oil pressure, compression, leaks.

Warranty

Disposal of old engine
Environmentally compliant disposal or recycling.

⚠️ What's NOT Included (Hidden Costs to Watch For)

🔗
Clutch and DMF (manual gearbox)
£400-£1,000 extra. While the engine is out, it's cheaper to replace them now.
💧
Water pump / thermostat
£100-£300 extra. Often recommended as a precaution while the engine is out.
❄️
Air-con regas
£50-£150 extra. Removing the engine requires disconnecting air-con pipes.
🔧
Ancillary parts
£200-£1,000+ extra. Your existing ancillaries may be worn and need replacing.
🔍
Unforeseen repairs
Varies. Worn engine mounts, exhaust leaks, corroded pipes, damaged wiring discovered during fitting.
🚗
Vehicle recovery
£100-£300+ extra. If your vehicle isn't drivable, it needs to be recovered to the garage.
The golden rule:

Ask every supplier for a full written breakdown of exactly what is and isn't included. Get it in writing before paying a deposit.

Warranty Coverage

Warranty - What's Covered

The warranty on a supply-and-fit job depends on the condition type you choose.

ConditionWarranty DurationCoverageTypical Exclusions
Used Engine1-6 monthsParts only (internal mechanical failure)Labour, ancillaries, gaskets, seals
Reconditioned Engine6-12 monthsParts & labour (internal mechanical failure)Ancillaries, gaskets, seals, consequential damage
Rebuilt Engine12-24 monthsParts & labour (internal mechanical failure)Ancillaries, gaskets, seals, consequential damage
Remanufactured Engine12-24 monthsParts & labour (internal mechanical failure)Ancillaries, gaskets, seals, consequential damage
✅ What the Warranty Typically Covers
  • Internal mechanical failure of the engine assembly (block, cylinder head, crankshaft, pistons, connecting rods, camshaft, valves, timing components)
  • Parts and labour (for reconditioned, rebuilt, and remanufactured)
  • Parts only (for used engines)
❌ What the Warranty Typically Does NOT Cover
  • Ancillaries (alternator, starter, turbo, injectors, fuel pump)
  • Belts and hoses
  • Gaskets and seals (if they fail after installation)
  • Damage from incorrect fitting, poor lubrication, or overheating after installation
  • Damage from neglect or failure to maintain
  • Consequential damage (e.g., component failure damaging other parts)
The Golden Rule:

Always read the warranty terms before committing. A warranty is only as good as the supplier standing behind it. If a supplier cannot tell you the claims process, the exclusions, and the duration, they are not offering a real warranty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about supply and fit

How much does supply and fit cost- ×
Supply-and-fit costs vary by condition type: Used £2,500-£4,500, Reconditioned £3,500-£5,500, Rebuilt £4,500-£7,500, Remanufactured £5,500-£9,000. Labour is the biggest variable - regional rates range from £65-£160/hr.
What's included in the fitting cost- +
Fitting typically includes: removal of the old engine, installation of the replacement engine, new consumables (oil, coolant, filters), basic gaskets and seals, post-installation testing, and disposal of the old engine. Always get a written breakdown.
How long does engine replacement take- +
The average timeline from quote request to completion is 5.2 days [EM-VERIFIED]. Labour typically takes 8-15 hours depending on engine type and vehicle complexity.
What warranty comes with supply and fit- +
The warranty depends on the condition type: Used 1-6 months (parts only), Reconditioned 6-12 months (parts & labour), Rebuilt 12-24 months (parts & labour), Remanufactured 12-24 months (parts & labour).
Do you offer vehicle recovery- +
Some suppliers offer recovery as part of the service. If not, you can arrange independent recovery or use your breakdown cover. We recommend confirming recovery options when you get your quotes.
Can I choose my own garage- +
If you choose supply-only, you can use any garage. If you choose supply-and-fit, the supplier will arrange fitting with one of their partner garages.
What if I already have my own garage- +
You can choose supply-only and have the engine delivered to your chosen garage. The supplier will provide the engine; your garage handles the fitting.
Do I need to tell the DVLA and my insurer- +
DVLA - update the engine number on your V5C [DVLA, 2025]. Insurer - you should check with your insurer. Some policies require notification of major mechanical changes, while others may not.
