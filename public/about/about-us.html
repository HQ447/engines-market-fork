What we do (and what we do not do)

A comparison platform - nothing more, nothing less

Engines Market is a price comparison platform. We connect you with independent UK engine suppliers. That is all.

We do
  • Help you compare quotes from vetted specialists
  • Provide transparent pricing guidance
  • Share marketplace data (e.g., 24,650+ quote requests)
  • Introduce you to relevant suppliers
We do NOT
  • Sell engines ourselves
  • Fit engines
  • Provide warranties
  • Handle payments
  • Mediate disputes between customers and suppliers

All contracts, warranties, and payments are directly between you and the supplier you choose. Our role is to give you the information and options you need to make a confident decision.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Engines Market

What does Engines Market do- +
Engines Market is a price comparison platform that connects you with independent UK engine suppliers. We help you compare quotes from vetted specialists to find a fair price for engine replacement.
How does the engine replacement quote process work- +
You submit your enquiry online. We match you with relevant specialists from our network of 100+ UK suppliers. You will typically receive 3-5 quotes within a few hours, then you choose the supplier that best fits your needs.
Is Engines Market a garage or engine seller- +
No. Engines Market is a comparison platform. We do not sell engines, fit engines, provide warranties, or handle payments. All contracts are directly between you and the supplier you choose.
How much does it cost to use Engines Market- +
It is completely free for drivers. We do not charge you anything to submit an enquiry or receive quotes. Our revenue comes from suppliers who pay a small fee to be part of our network.
How do you vet your suppliers- +
We vet suppliers based on their UK business registration, trading history, customer reviews, and adherence to our supplier standards. We regularly review supplier performance to maintain quality.
