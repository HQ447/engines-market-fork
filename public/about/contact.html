Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I visit your office or workshop-

No. Engines Market is an online marketplace. We do not have a public-facing reception, and we do not carry out any mechanical work. All engine supply and fitting is handled directly by the suppliers in our network.

How do I get a quote for my engine replacement-

Please use our quote form. Our support team cannot provide pricing over the phone - we need your vehicle registration and failure details to match you with relevant suppliers.

I am a supplier - how do I join your network-

Email suppliers@enginesmarket.co.uk with your company name, registered address, and a link to your public reviews. We will send you our onboarding criteria.

I have a complaint about a supplier I found through Engines Market.

We are sorry to hear that. Please first contact the supplier directly to give them the opportunity to resolve the issue. If you are still unhappy, email support@enginesmarket.co.uk with your order reference and details. We do not mediate disputes, but we track complaints as part of our supplier monitoring and removal process.
