Here is exactly how it works

The process in four steps

We believe getting a replacement engine should be straightforward. Here is a step-by-step walkthrough of what happens when you use Engines Market.

1
Submit your enquiry
Tell us about your vehicle and the problem. You will need:
  • Vehicle registration number
  • Current mileage
  • Brief description of the issue
The enquiry form takes about two minutes to complete. It is completely free and does not commit you to anything.
2
We match you with relevant suppliers
Once you submit your enquiry, we share it - anonymously - with specialist suppliers in our network who are a good fit for your vehicle and location.
Our network includes 100+ UK engine specialists that have been onboarded against our supplier standards. Generally, they must be UK registered, have a physical business address, a minimum one-year trading history, and a public review profile averaging approximately 4.0 stars or higher.
Only suppliers who can genuinely help with your engine type will receive your enquiry. We do not send leads to everyone indiscriminately.
3
Receive and compare quotes
Suppliers review your details and send you a quote directly - usually by email or phone.
  • Typical number of quotes: 3 to 5
  • Typical time to receive quotes: Within a few hours
You can compare prices, warranty terms, and included services side by side. All quotes come from real UK businesses, not middlemen.
Based on 24,650 quote requests processed in 2025 [EM-VERIFIED], most users receive multiple competitive offers without needing to chase suppliers.
4
You choose the supplier - or walk away
There is no pressure. You decide:
  • Which supplier to contact for more details
  • Whether to accept a quote
  • Whether to walk away and do nothing
Engines Market does not choose a supplier for you. We do not take a cut of the transaction. Your relationship - including payment, warranty, and fitting - is directly with the supplier you select.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the process

Is the service really free- +
Yes. You pay nothing to use Engines Market. Our income comes from suppliers who pay for qualified leads.
How do you vet suppliers- +
Suppliers generally need a UK registered business, a physical address, at least one year of trading history, and a public review profile averaging around 4.0 stars or higher. See our full supplier standards page for details.
How long does the whole process take- +
Most users receive quotes within a few hours. From enquiry to completed engine replacement, the average timeline (based on our 2025 data) is 5.2 days - but this varies by supplier and vehicle.
What if I do not like any of the quotes- +
You are free to walk away. There is no obligation to accept any quote or to use our service again.
Do you guarantee the lowest price- +
No. We provide multiple quotes from real suppliers so you can compare. The best choice depends on price, warranty, location, and your own priorities.
