What this usually means in UK cars

Your engine's temperature warning system

The coolant warning light is your engine's temperature warning system. Unlike the red oil light (which means immediate danger), the coolant light can mean anything from a minor top-up to catastrophic overheating - depending on which light you see.

In our 24,650 quote dataset, we identified four primary causes:

Low coolant level - 50% of cases

Simple top-up required. Cost: £5-£150.

Coolant leak - 30% of cases

External leak from hose, radiator, or water pump. Cost: £50-£700.

Thermostat failure - 12% of cases

Stuck closed, causing overheating. Cost: £80-£300.

Head gasket failure - 8% of cases

Coolant entering combustion chamber. Cost: £550-£2,000+.

What people think vs reality

The expensive mistake is ignoring the light

“The light came on but the gauge is fine - I'll keep driving.”
The coolant light is a warning. The gauge is the confirmation. Don't ignore the light.
“I'll just top up the coolant when I get home.”
Coolant loss while driving can lead to rapid overheating. Stop and check.
“Opening the coolant reservoir is fine when hot.”
NEVER open the coolant reservoir when hot. Pressurised coolant causes severe burns.
“I'll just use water - it's fine.”
Water isn't coolant. It boils at a lower temperature and lacks corrosion inhibitors. Use proper coolant.
“The light came on once and went off - it's fine.”
Intermittent lights indicate intermittent problems. The fault is still there.

⚠️ Red thermometer = stop driving immediately

If the red thermometer light comes on:

  • Pull over safely - Find a safe location immediately
  • Turn off engine - Do not wait. Turn off the key immediately
  • Wait 30+ minutes - Do not open the bonnet. Coolant is pressurised
  • Do not add coolant - Wait for the engine to cool completely
  • Call for recovery - Do not drive

Why this is critical:

  • The red thermometer means the engine is overheating
  • Overheating causes cylinder head warping, head gasket failure, and piston seizure
  • Continuing to drive will destroy the engine

Check when cool:

  • Never open the coolant reservoir when the engine is hot
  • Pressurised coolant can spray out and cause severe burns
  • Wait at least 30 minutes before checking

🟡 Yellow coolant light (waves) = Check when cool

The yellow waves light means low coolant level. Check the level when the engine is cold. Top up to the correct level. Monitor for future loss (indicates a leak).

What to do next

Identify, check, diagnose

  • Step 1: Identify which light is on
    Red thermometer- → STOP DRIVING
    Yellow/red waves- → Check coolant level (when cold)
  • Step 2: Check coolant level (when cold)
    Wait 30+ minutes after driving · Open the coolant reservoir slowly (cover with a cloth) · Is the level between min and max-
  • Step 3: If coolant level is low
    Top up to the correct level · Use the correct coolant type for your vehicle · Monitor for future loss
  • Step 4: If coolant level drops again
    Book a diagnostic (£50-£150) - there is a leak in the system
  • Step 5: Professional diagnostic (£50-£150)
    A technician will pressure test the cooling system, check for exhaust gases in coolant (head gasket test), inspect for external leaks, and test thermostat and water pump function.

Frequently asked questions

Coolant warning light FAQs

What does the red thermometer light mean-

Engine overheating. Stop driving immediately.

What does the yellow coolant wave light mean-

Low coolant level. Check the level when the engine is cold.

Can I open the coolant reservoir when the engine is hot-

No. Pressurised coolant can spray out and cause severe burns. Wait 30+ minutes.

How much does a coolant leak repair cost in the UK-

Coolant leak repair costs £50-£700 depending on the cause. Hoses: £50-£200. Radiator: £300-£800.

Is the coolant light the same as the temperature gauge-

They are related. The coolant light is a warning. The temperature gauge shows the actual temperature. If both indicate a problem - stop immediately.
