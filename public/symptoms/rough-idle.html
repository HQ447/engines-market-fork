Four primary causes of rough idle

A rough idle is one of the most common engine complaints - and one of the easiest to diagnose with the right approach.

In our 24,650 quote dataset, we identified four primary causes:

  • Ignition system (35% of cases) → Spark plugs, coils, leads. Repair: £60-£220.
  • Vacuum leaks (25% of cases) → Hoses, gaskets, intake manifold. Repair: £100-£400.
  • Fuel system (20% of cases) → Injectors, fuel pressure. Repair: £100-£900.
  • Compression issues (15% of cases) → Valves, piston rings. Repair: £500-£2,000+.
  • Sensor failure (5% of cases) → MAF, oxygen sensors. Repair: £100-£300.

The key pattern: If the idle improves when you drive, it's typically ignition or vacuum. If it gets worse when warm, suspect compression or valve issues.

What people think vs reality

Don't fall for these common misconceptions

“It's just a dirty air filter - cheap fix.”
Dirty air filters rarely cause rough idle. It's usually ignition or vacuum.
“I'll just drive it - it'll clear.”
Rough idle won't clear itself. It will get worse and cause misfires.
“A fuel system cleaner will fix it.”
Sometimes - if it's a minor injector issue. But most cases need proper repairs.
“Rough idle means a big engine problem.”
Usually not. 80% of rough idle cases are simple fixes (£60-£400).

⚠️ WHEN ROUGH IDLE NEEDS URGENT ATTENTION

Rough idle is rarely an emergency - but these symptoms require prompt action:

  • Flashing engine light → Active misfire - STOP DRIVING
  • Rough idle + loss of power → Multiple causes
  • Rough idle + stalling → Safety issue
  • Rough idle + overheating → Head gasket risk

The Flashing Light Danger: If your check engine light is flashing, unburnt fuel is entering the exhaust system and destroying your catalytic converter (£500-£2,500 repair).

Frequently asked questions

Rough idle FAQs

How much does rough idle diagnosis cost in the UK-

Rough idle diagnosis costs £50-£150 at UK independent garages. This includes fault code reading and basic inspection.

Can rough idle damage my car-

Yes - if it's caused by a misfire, unburnt fuel can damage the catalytic converter (£500-£2,500).

Is rough idle an MOT failure-

Yes if it affects emissions or if the EML is on.

Can a fuel system cleaner fix rough idle-

Sometimes - if it's a minor injector issue. But most cases need proper repairs.
