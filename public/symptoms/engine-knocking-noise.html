What this usually means in UK cars

Rod knock is the most serious engine noise you can hear

A deep, rhythmic knock that increases with engine RPM is rod knock. It means the bearing between the connecting rod and crankshaft has failed. Oil pressure loss is imminent. Driving for even a few minutes will destroy the crankshaft and potentially the entire engine block.

In our 24,650 quote dataset, 87% of rod knock cases resulted in engine replacement because drivers continued running the engine after symptoms appeared.

What people think vs reality

The expensive mistake is assuming it is harmless

“It's just a timing issue - I can drive it to the garage.”
If it's rod knock, the bearing is already failing. Each revolution causes more damage. Driving even 1 mile turns an £800 repair into a £5,000+ replacement.
“I'll add thicker oil to quieten it.”
Thicker oil masks the noise briefly while continuing to destroy the bearing. You're silencing the symptom, not fixing the problem.
“My car has done 100k miles; this is normal wear.”
Rod knock is not normal at any mileage. It's a failure of the lubrication system or bearing material.
“A mechanic can fix this with an engine flush.”
Engine flushes do not fix mechanical bearing failure. At best, they clean the system; at worst, they accelerate failure.

What to do next

Three steps to protect your engine

  • Step 1: Immediate Safety Stop (£0) - Turn off the engine immediately. Do not restart until assessment.
  • Step 2: Professional Diagnostic (£50-£150) - A technician will use a stethoscope to pinpoint the noise, check oil pressure and condition, remove and inspect the oil filter for metal particles, and perform a compression test if required.
  • Step 3: Decision Point - If caught early: bearing replacement (engine-out labour). If crankshaft damaged: crankshaft replacement or engine replacement. If block damaged: engine replacement.

What UK specialists say

Common affected engines and root causes

According to UK independent specialist consensus:

  • Rod knock is most common in high-mileage vehicles (80,000+ miles) with irregular oil changes.
  • Oil pump failure causes rod knock: VAG 1.8T/2.0T and BMW N20 engines are known for oil pump issues.
  • Low oil levels are a primary cause: 60% of rod knock cases occur in vehicles that were 1+ litres low on oil.
  • Any engine can suffer rod knock - proper oil maintenance is your only defence.

Frequently asked questions

Engine knocking noise FAQs

What's the difference between engine knocking and rattling-

Knocking is a deep, rhythmic "thud" that increases with engine RPM. Rattling is a metallic, higher-pitched sound. Knocking indicates bearing failure - more serious.

Can low oil cause engine knocking-

Yes. Low oil causes insufficient lubrication, leading to bearing wear and ultimately rod knock. Check your oil level immediately.

Is detonation the same as rod knock-

No. Detonation sounds like marbles rattling and occurs under acceleration. Rod knock is a deep rhythmic knock at idle that worsens with RPM.

How much does rod knock repair cost in the UK-

£800-£1,500 if caught early (bearing replacement only). £2,500-£10,000+ if crankshaft or block is damaged.
