The most common - and most misunderstood - warning light

The check engine light (EML) is the most common warning light - and the most misunderstood. It can mean anything from a loose fuel cap (£0) to a failing catalytic converter (£500-£2,500).

In our 24,650 quote dataset, we identified five primary causes:

Loose or faulty fuel cap - 20% of cases

EVAP system leak. Cost: £0-£30.

Oxygen sensor failure - 25% of cases

Lambda sensor reading incorrect. Cost: £100-£300.

Ignition system failure - 20% of cases

Spark plugs, coils, leads. Cost: £60-£220.

Catalytic converter failure - 15% of cases

Blocked or damaged cat. Cost: £500-£2,500+.

MAF sensor / EVAP - 20% of cases

Air flow readings incorrect or fuel vapour leak. Cost: £50-£300.

⚠️ Flashing check engine light - stop driving

If your check engine light is flashing:

  • Pull over safely - Find a safe location
  • Turn off engine - Do not wait
  • Call for recovery - Do not drive

Why this is critical:

  • A flashing light means the engine is misfiring
  • Unburnt fuel is entering the exhaust system
  • This fuel destroys the catalytic converter (£500-£2,500)
  • Continuing to drive turns a £100 ignition repair into a £2,500 cat replacement

The loose fuel cap rule

Always check your fuel cap first

💡 Always check your fuel cap first.

  • A loose cap triggers the EVAP system code (P0455/P0457)
  • This is the most common EML trigger (20% of cases)
  • Tighten the cap and drive for 50 miles
  • The light will usually clear

Why this works:

  • The EVAP system monitors fuel vapour leaks
  • A loose cap causes a vapour leak
  • The ECU detects the leak and illuminates the light
  • Tightening the cap seals the leak
  • The light clears after several drive cycles

If the light doesn't clear after 50 miles: Book a diagnostic - there's another issue.

Frequently asked questions

Check engine light FAQs

What does the check engine light mean-

It means the ECU has detected a fault in the engine or emissions system. It could be minor (fuel cap) or serious (misfire).

Can I drive with the check engine light on-

Solid amber - yes, short distances. Flashing or red - no, stop immediately.

How much does check engine light diagnosis cost in the UK-

£50-£150 at UK independent garages. This includes reading fault codes.

Why is the check engine light flashing-

Active misfire. Unburnt fuel is destroying your catalytic converter. Stop driving immediately.

How long does it take for the check engine light to clear after fixing-

50-100 miles of driving, or the garage can clear it with diagnostic equipment.

What's the most common cause of a check engine light-

A loose fuel cap (20% of cases). Always check it first.
