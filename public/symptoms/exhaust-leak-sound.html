What this usually means in UK cars

An exhaust leak isn't just about noise - it can be dangerous

An exhaust leak isn't just about noise - it can be dangerous. Leaks before the catalytic converter or oxygen sensor can affect emissions, fuel economy, and potentially allow carbon monoxide into the cabin.

In our 24,650 quote dataset, we identified four primary causes:

  • Manifold gasket leak (25% of cases) - Ticking noise from engine bay. Cost: £100-£300.
  • Exhaust pipe leak (30% of cases) - Hissing noise under the car. Cost: £25-£600.
  • Back box/silencer failure (25% of cases) - Rumbling/booming noise. Cost: £80-£300.
  • Catalytic converter failure (20% of cases) - Rattling and emissions light. Cost: £500-£2,500+.

The critical rule: "Any exhaust leak is an MOT failure. The bigger concern is carbon monoxide entering the cabin."

⚠️ CARBON MONOXIDE WARNING - DANGEROUS

If you smell exhaust fumes inside the car:

  • OPEN WINDOWS IMMEDIATELY - Get fresh air into the cabin
  • PULL OVER SAFELY - Find a safe location
  • DO NOT DRIVE - Arrange recovery
  • GET IT REPAIRED - Do not drive until the leak is fixed

Why This Is Critical:

  • Carbon monoxide (CO) is odourless and colourless
  • If you can smell exhaust fumes, CO is present
  • CO poisoning causes headaches, dizziness, nausea, confusion, and death
  • Children and pets are more susceptible
  • Never drive with a leak that allows fumes into the cabin

Safety Warning: "If you smell exhaust fumes inside the car, there is a leak allowing carbon monoxide into the cabin. CO is odourless and colourless but extremely dangerous. Open windows immediately and arrange repair."

MOT Implications: "Any visible exhaust leak or excessive noise from the exhaust system is an automatic MOT failure."

Frequently asked questions

Exhaust leak sound FAQs

How much does exhaust leak diagnosis cost in the UK-

Exhaust leak diagnosis costs £50-£150 at UK independent garages. This includes visual inspection and smoke test.

Can I drive with an exhaust leak-

If the leak is before the cat or oxygen sensor, it can affect emissions. If you smell fumes inside - stop immediately.

Is an exhaust leak an MOT failure-

Yes. Any visible exhaust leak or excessive noise is an automatic MOT failure.

How much does back box replacement cost in the UK-

Back box replacement costs £80-£300 depending on the vehicle and whether it's aftermarket or OEM.

Is exhaust repair paste a permanent fix-

No. It's temporary. If the pipe is corroded, it needs proper replacement.
