The key question: does it crank or is there silence-

An engine that won't start is one of the most stressful situations for any driver. But here's the crucial distinction:

It cranks but won't start → £100-£900

Fuel, spark, or compression issue. Usually fixable.

It doesn't crank → £60-£400

Starter motor or battery issue. Usually fixable.

It won't turn at all → £1,500-£8,000+

Engine seized. This is the nightmare scenario.

In our 24,650 quote dataset, 70% of no-start cases were battery, starter, or alternator issues - not catastrophic engine failure. However, 10% were seized engines, and these are the cases that require replacement.

What people think vs reality

The expensive mistake is assuming it's just the battery

“It's just the battery - I'll jump start it.”
Jump starts can mask the problem. If it doesn't start after 3 attempts, stop before you damage the starter.
“Keep trying - it'll eventually start.”
Repeated attempts flood petrol engines and can burn out starter motors.
“If it cranks, the battery is fine.”
Not necessarily. Weak batteries can crank slowly but lack voltage for the ECU/injectors.
“A seized engine means I need a new car.”
Not always - but you'll need a replacement engine (£1,500-£8,000) or a new car.

⚠️ Seized engine warning - stop attempting to start

If you turn the key and absolutely nothing happens - the engine may be seized. Seized engines cannot be repaired - only replaced.

Signs of a seized engine:

  • Silence when you turn the key (no crank, no click)
  • Starter motor engages but engine doesn't turn
  • Engine locked solid - won't turn with a breaker bar
  • Previous symptoms - overheating, oil light, knocking before failure

Do not keep trying to start a seized engine. You'll damage the starter motor and flywheel.

✅ DO:

Stop attempting to start · Arrange recovery to a garage · Get a diagnostic to confirm

❌ DON'T:

Keep trying · Jump start · Push start (will damage transmission)

What to do next

Don't panic - listen carefully

  • Step 1: Don't panic - listen carefully
    Turn the key and listen: Clicking → Battery issue. No sound → Starter or seized engine. Cranking sound → Fuel/spark/compression issue.
  • Step 2: Check the battery
    Do lights come on- Are they dim- Try jump starting (if safe). If jump starting works → battery is the issue.
  • Step 3: Call a professional (£50-£150)
    A technician will test battery voltage and condition, check starter motor function, check fuel pressure and spark, test compression if required, and confirm if engine is seized.

Frequently asked questions

Engine won't start FAQs

What does it mean if my engine won't turn over-

If the engine doesn't crank when you turn the key, it's either a dead battery, failed starter motor, or a seized engine. The most expensive is a seized engine.

Can a seized engine be fixed-

No. Seized engines cannot be repaired - only replaced. The internal damage is too extensive.

How much does a starter motor replacement cost in the UK-

Starter motor replacement costs £100-£400 depending on the vehicle. Labour accounts for most of the cost.

How much does an engine replacement cost in the UK-

Engine replacement costs £1,500-£8,000+ depending on the vehicle and whether you choose used, rebuilt, or new.
