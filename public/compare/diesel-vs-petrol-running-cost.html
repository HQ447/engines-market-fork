EEAT anchor · what people think vs reality

The truth about diesel vs petrol costs

Many owners assume diesel is always cheaper because it's more fuel efficient. Here's why that's not always true.

❌ What many owners think

"Diesel is always cheaper because it's more fuel efficient."

✅ Reality

Diesel fuel economy is better - but the total cost of ownership is not always lower. Higher repair costs (DPF, EGR, AdBlue), ULEZ/CAZ charges (£12.50/day), higher purchase price, and declining resale value all erode diesel savings.

"In real UK driving conditions, diesel only makes financial sense for high-mileage, motorway-based drivers. For urban drivers - particularly those in ULEZ/CAZ zones - petrol is often cheaper despite lower fuel economy. The DPF, EGR, and AdBlue costs erode the fuel saving." - Engines Market, [EM-OBSERVED]
Real-world example (15,000 miles/year): Petrol fuel cost £2,045; diesel £1,386 - saving £659. But ULEZ/CAZ charges (if non-compliant) add £3,000+, and DPF/AdBlue issues add £200-£1,500. Net annual cost: petrol £2,045 vs diesel £2,586-£5,886.

Decision framework · rules-based logic

Choose petrol or diesel with confidence

Use these rules to guide your decision. The choice depends on annual mileage, driving type, and ULEZ/CAZ compliance.

✅ Choose PETROL if:
  • • Driving mostly short trips (<10 miles)
  • • Live in/near ULEZ/CAZ with pre-Euro 6 diesel
  • • Annual mileage under 12,000 miles
  • • Vehicle is older (pre-2016)
  • • Want simpler emissions system (no DPF, EGR, AdBlue)
  • • Vehicle value under £5,000
Default: Petrol if urban driving, under 12k miles/year, or ULEZ/CAZ area.
✅ Choose DIESEL if:
  • • High-mileage driver (15,000+ miles/year)
  • • Motorway/A-road commutes (DPF regenerates properly)
  • • Low ULEZ/CAZ risk (rural or outside clean air zones)
  • • Vehicle is Euro 6 (2016+) - ULEZ/CAZ compliant
  • • Need towing capability (diesel torque)
  • • Commercial use - high mileage, long-distance, load carrying
Default: Diesel if 15k+ miles/year, motorway driving, and ULEZ/CAZ compliant.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about diesel vs petrol

Which is cheaper to run - diesel or petrol- +
It depends on your driving. Diesel has better fuel economy (45-65 mpg vs 30-45 mpg) and lower fuel cost per mile. But diesel engines cost more to maintain (DPF, EGR, AdBlue, timing chain) and face ULEZ/CAZ charges if non-compliant. For high-mileage motorway drivers (15k+ miles/year), diesel is cheaper. For urban drivers with short trips, petrol is cheaper.
Is diesel still worth it with ULEZ charges- +
Only if your diesel is Euro 6 (2016+) and ULEZ/CAZ compliant. Pre-Euro 6 diesels face £12.50/day ULEZ or £10/day CAZ charges - which can exceed £3,000/year for daily users. If you live in or near a clean air zone and don't have a Euro 6 diesel, petrol is almost always cheaper.
What mileage should I switch from petrol to diesel- +
The breakeven point is typically 12,000-15,000 miles/year. Below 12,000 miles, petrol is usually cheaper. Above 15,000 miles, diesel starts to make financial sense - especially if you do motorway driving where DPF regenerates properly. Between 12,000 and 15,000 miles, evaluate your specific driving pattern.
Why do diesel engines cost more to replace- +
Diesel engines have more complex and expensive components: turbocharger, high-pressure injectors, DPF, EGR valve, AdBlue system, and often more labour-intensive timing chains (e.g., BMW N47, Land Rover Ingenium). A reconditioned diesel 4-cylinder costs £700-£1,400 more than the petrol equivalent.
What is DPF and why is it a problem- +
DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter) traps soot particles from diesel exhaust. It regenerates (burns off soot) during long motorway journeys. On short trips, regeneration fails and the DPF blocks - costing £150-£600 to clean or £400-£1,200 to replace. This is why diesel is not recommended for urban drivers.
What is Euro 6 and why does it matter- +
Euro 6 is the EU emissions standard introduced in 2016. Diesel vehicles must be Euro 6 compliant to enter ULEZ and most CAZ zones without charges. Pre-Euro 6 diesels (2015 and earlier) face daily charges. Petrol vehicles need Euro 4 (2006+) for ULEZ compliance.
Does a diesel save money on fuel- +
Yes - diesel is typically 12-16p per mile vs 16-20p for petrol. At 15,000 miles/year, diesel saves £500-£1,000/year in fuel. But this saving can be eroded by higher repair costs, ULEZ charges, and declining resale value. Calculate your total cost of ownership, not just fuel cost.
What's the resale value of diesel vs petrol- +
Petrol resale value is stable. Diesel resale value is declining, especially for pre-Euro 6 diesels, due to ULEZ/CAZ restrictions and changing buyer preferences. High-mileage diesels are particularly difficult to sell.
Can I still buy a diesel car in 2026- +
Yes - but only if it's Euro 6 compliant and you drive enough miles (15k+) to justify the higher costs. For urban drivers, petrol is almost always the better choice. New diesel car sales are declining, but used diesels remain widely available.
What's the best fuel type for short trips- +
Petrol is strongly recommended for short trips. Diesel engines need long motorway journeys for DPF regeneration; short trips cause DPF clogging and expensive repairs. Petrol engines have simpler emissions systems and are more reliable for urban driving.
