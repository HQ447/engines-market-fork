What people think vs reality

The misconception that costs owners thousands

❌ What many owners think

"Main dealers are more expensive but better quality."

✅ Reality

Main dealers are more expensive - but not necessarily better quality for engine replacement. Independent specialists often have more experienced technicians on complex engine work.

Why independent specialists often deliver better value:
• More experienced technicians - specialists work on the same engines every day
• Better value parts - reconditioned and remanufactured options vs expensive new OEM
• Faster turnaround - less bureaucracy, more flexibility
• Direct supplier relationships - access to the same reconditioned engine network
• Fixed-fee pricing - no surprise labour hours

Decision framework - rules-based logic

Choose with confidence

Use the frameworks below to make the right choice for your vehicle.

Choose MAIN DEALER if:

  • ✅ Vehicle under manufacturer warranty
  • ✅ Insurance job paying full cost
  • ✅ Manufacturer goodwill contribution possible
  • ✅ You want OEM parts only
  • ✅ Vehicle is less than 3 years old
  • ✅ You value dealer loan car
  • ✅ You want OEM warranty (12-36 months)
Default: Main dealer if under warranty, insurance-funded, or manufacturer goodwill available.

Choose INDEPENDENT SPECIALIST if:

  • ✅ Out of warranty
  • ✅ Want recon/remanufactured (saving £500-£4,000+)
  • ✅ Paying out of pocket
  • ✅ Complex engine work (specialists do it daily)
  • ✅ Vehicle is 5+ years old
  • ✅ You want fixed-fee pricing
  • ✅ Faster turnaround needed
Default: Independent specialist if out of warranty and paying out of pocket.
