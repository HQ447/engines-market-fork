EEAT anchor - myth vs reality

What people think vs what really happens

❌ What many owners think:

"Supply only is cheaper because I can find a cheaper garage."

✅ Reality:

Supply only may be cheaper upfront - but the total cost often exceeds supply & fit when you factor in:

  • Garage markup on the engine (some garages add 10-20% to supply-only engines)
  • Consumables (oil, coolant, filters, gaskets - often £200-£500 extra)
  • Collection/delivery of the engine to your garage (£50-£200)
  • Disposal of the old engine (£50-£150)
  • Diagnostic time if issues arise during fitting
  • Warranty disputes - if the engine fails, the supplier blames the fitter and vice versa

"In real UK workshop practice, supply & fit packages often offer better value than separate arrangements when you factor in consumables, collection, disposal, and the simplified liability of a single point of contact." [EM-OBSERVED]

Real-world example:
Supply only: £2,000 engine + £800 labour + £300 consumables + £150 collection/disposal = £3,250
Supply & fit: £3,000 (often lower than the sum of separate parts)
